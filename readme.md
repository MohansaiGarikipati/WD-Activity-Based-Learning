## Selected 3 Case Studies

1. **Online Shopping Website**
   - **Overview:** An e-commerce platform designed for browsing products, managing a shopping cart, and processing online orders securely.
   - **Key Features:** Product catalog, search and filtering, shopping cart functionality, user authentication, and checkout/payment integration.

2. **Fitness Centre and Gym Website**
   - **Overview:** A web portal for a local fitness center to showcase facilities, membership plans, and trainer profiles.
   - **Key Features:** Class schedules, online membership registration, personal trainer booking system, and interactive fitness blogs/tips.

3. **Online Food and Nutrition Website**
   - **Overview:** A digital platform focused on healthy eating, providing meal delivery plans, calorie tracking, and nutritional advice.
   - **Key Features:** Customized diet plan selection, interactive meal menu, nutrition tracking calculator, and online ordering system.
